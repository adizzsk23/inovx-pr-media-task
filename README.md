## The Two Tasks

Every member does **both** tasks.

### 1. Media — reveal animation (individual submission)

Build a reveal animation for your panel's assigned subject. Logo assets will be shared separately.

- **AI tools are allowed** for this task.
- Judged on creativity and a premium, brand-grade finish. This should look like something the club would actually publish.
- Drawing from past INOVX posts or going fully original are both fine.
- Every member submits their own animation. This is not a group deliverable.

### 2. PR — outreach strategy (team task, split into parts)

Build a complete on-campus and online outreach strategy for your panel's assigned event, covering the whole funnel — from reaching out to sponsors all the way through to getting people to actually register.

**On-campus:** creative activities and ground-level ideas. Be specific and executable. "Put up posters" is not a plan; what the poster does, where it goes, and what makes someone stop is.

**Online:** if you propose reels, give the actual hook ideas — the first three seconds, written out. If you propose posts, give the niche, the design pattern, and the visual language. Naming a channel is not a strategy.

Everything, online and offline, must be documented and submitted.

**How the panel splits it:** divide the work by part so every piece has a named owner — for example sponsor outreach, inter-college outreach, on-campus activation, online content. Agree on the split early. Coordination is tracked through this repository's commit history, so **each person's work must show up under their own name** — upload your own part yourself, from your own GitHub account. Do not have one person upload on behalf of the panel.

## Rules

- **AI:** allowed for Media. **Not recommended for PR** — the strategy work is what we are actually reading, and it shows.
- **Inspiration is fine, copying is not.** Looking at past INOVX posts to understand the tone is encouraged. Reproducing someone else's work is not.
- **Media is individual. PR is teamwork, but each person submits their own part.**

## Bonus

Panels that actually shoot and edit their own video content earn extra credit — for PR, and for Media where possible. Goofy reels are welcome as part of the PR work. Shooting something real counts for more than describing something polished.

## How to Upload

Two separate uploads, into two different folders. Read the section for each.

Before you start: **use a computer, not the GitHub phone app** — the mobile app cannot upload files. A phone browser can work in desktop mode, but a laptop is far less painful.

### Uploading your PR part

1. Sign in to GitHub. If you do not have an account, create a free one at github.com first.
2. Open this repository page and click the `PR` folder.
3. Click your panel's folder — `Panel-1` or `Panel-2`.
4. Near the top right of the file list, click the **Add file** button, then choose **Upload files**.
5. Drag in your part as a **single PDF**, named `Panel1_YourName_Part.pdf` — using your panel number, your name, and the part you owned. For example: `Panel1_Tejas_SponsorOutreach.pdf`.
6. Scroll to the bottom, to the **Commit changes** box. Leave the default message or type something like `Add Panel1_Tejas_SponsorOutreach.pdf`.
7. Click the green **Commit changes** button.
8. The page reloads and your PDF appears in the folder. That means it is submitted.

### Uploading your Media animation

1. From the repository page, click the `Media` folder, then your panel's folder — `Panel-1` or `Panel-2`.
2. Click **Add file**, then **Upload files**.
3. **If your export is under 25 MB:** drag in the video itself, named `Panel1_YourName.mp4`. 25 MB is GitHub's hard limit for uploading through the browser — a larger file will simply be rejected.
4. **If it is 25 MB or larger:** do not upload the video, it will not go through. Instead upload a plain text file named `Panel1_YourName_link.txt` containing a shareable link to it — Google Drive, or anything similar. Most animation exports will land here, so expect to use this route.
5. **If you upload a link, turn view access on.** Set the link so that anyone with it can view. A link we cannot open counts as nothing submitted. Check it yourself in a private browser window before the deadline.
6. Scroll down and click the green **Commit changes** button.

## Judging Criteria

- **Creativity and originality** — whether the idea is yours and whether it is worth looking at twice.
- **Premium finish** — execution quality. Does it look like the club would publish it as is.
- **Strategy depth and specificity** — real hooks and real niches, not a list of vague channels.
- **Format sense** — each piece built for where it would actually run.
- **Coordination and teamwork** — whether the panel's parts add up to one strategy instead of four unrelated documents.
- **The video bonus** — see [Bonus](#bonus) above.
